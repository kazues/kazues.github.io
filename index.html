About

I am an Earth scientist working on polar meteorology and snow and ice science. My research combines in-situ and satellite observations of snowfall, snow cover, clouds and aerosols, with statistical and machine-learning methods used as tools for analysing these observations. I lead my own research projects as Principal Investigator.

Research fields

  • Polar meteorology: Antarctic snowfall and surface mass balance, aerosol transport over Antarctica
  • Snow and ice science: snow crystals, snowpack metamorphism, snow impurities, ice-core dating (Dome Fuji)
  • Observation and instrument development: automatic snow-crystal imaging, self-built automatic weather stations, microwave radiometer observations of precipitable water vapor
  • Data-driven analysis of observations: cloud and snowfall pattern detection, anomaly detection, Bayesian dating, emulation of a snowpack model
